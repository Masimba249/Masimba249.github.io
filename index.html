01About

Research profile

I am an embedded robotics engineer: I work where robot software meets the hardware that moves — microcontrollers, servo drives, motion-control firmware and real-time industrial fieldbuses. At Synapticon GmbH in Germany I test PID and field-oriented control firmware for servo drives and the Safe Motion Module drives of the Positron humanoid, using ROS2, hardware-in-the-loop benches, EtherCAT and JTAG debugging.

Before that, as a research assistant at Kazakh-British Technical University, I brought learning-based control onto real platforms — KUKA manipulators, quadcopter UAVs and PLC-coordinated multi-robot cells — work that led to two IEEE conference papers (IECON 2026, ICCA 2026). I am seeking a PhD position on embedded and real-time robot control: how learned controllers can run safely and deterministically on resource-constrained robotic hardware.
